As part of the **9th Artificial Intelligence in Chemistry Symposium**  
([AiChem 9 conference website](https://www.rscbmcs.org/events/aichem9/)), Ana and Lisa participated in both the pre-conference workshop and the main conference, contributing to discussions at the intersection of chemistry, data science, and machine learning.

Ana, Lisa, Andrea and [Pat](https://patwalters.github.io/) **organized the pre-conference workshop**, which introduced participants to practical machine learning workflows for chemistry. Lisa covered the fundamentals of **Python and Jupyter notebooks**, along with  **molecular representations and machine learning models** commonly used in cheminformatics and **conformal prediction**. Ana led a hands-on session on **data curation and cleaning** as well as **active learning**, giving participants the opportunity to explore these concepts through interactive notebooks. The workshop lectures and notebooks are publicly available 
([GitHub repository](https://github.com/volkamerlab/ai_in_chemistry_workshop_2026)).

During the main conference, Lisa presented her research in a **flash talk** presentation aswell as a poster titled **“To Strong Performance and Beyond: Trustworthy Machine Learning for Toxicity Prediction”**.

We especially enjoyed being part of the AiChem community, exchanging ideas, and connecting with researchers working across **machine learning, cheminformatics, and data-driven drug discovery**.
