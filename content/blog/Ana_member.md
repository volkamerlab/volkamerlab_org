date: 2026-04-01

Ana holds a bachelor's degree in applied chemistry and a dual master's degree in chemoinformatics and bioorganic chemistry from the Erasmus Mundus Cheminformatics+ partnership between the NOVA University of Lisbon and the University of Strasbourg. 
She joined our lab in March 2026 as a PhD candidate to be part of the EU-funded RADAR project.
She will work on the AI-based design of safe molecules following the SSbD framework.
