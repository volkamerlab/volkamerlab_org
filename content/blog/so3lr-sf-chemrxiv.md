title: "SO3LR-SF is out: a physics-based, explainable scoring function for protein–ligand binding"
date: 2026-09-08
author: hamza.agha
---

Our preprint "Explainable ML force-field for evaluating protein–ligand binding energy using SO3LR" is now out on [ChemRxiv](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv.15008482/v1)!

In our [_SO3LR-SF_](/projects/so3lr-sf/) project, we introduce a fast and explainable scoring function for protein–ligand binding built on the pretrained SO3LR machine-learned force field. It brings near-quantum-mechanical accuracy to structure-based drug design at a fraction of the cost, and shows *why* a ligand scores the way it does. The code is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/volkamerlab/so3lr-sf).

Many thanks to our collaborators Sergio Suárez-Dou, Adil Kabylda, and Alexandre Tkatchenko (University of Luxembourg) for working together on this project.
