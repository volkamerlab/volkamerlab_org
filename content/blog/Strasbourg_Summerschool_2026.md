As part of the **10th Strasbourg Summer School in Chemoinformatics (CS3-2026)**  
([CS3-2026 conference website](https://infochim.u-strasbg.fr/-strasbourg-summer-school-in-chemoinformatics-2026-.html)), Andrea, Walid, and Ana represented our team at the University of Strasbourg from June 22 to 26, 2026. The summer school brought together students, early-career researchers, and experienced scientists to discuss recent developments in chemoinformatics, artificial intelligence in chemistry, chemical space exploration, QSAR modeling, and computer-aided drug design.

Andrea delivered a talk titled **“Data-Driven Exploration of Kinase Inhibitor Space: AI-Assisted, Structure-Based, and Fragment-Guided Discovery”**.
([talk abstract](https://infochim.u-strasbg.fr/IMG/pdf/abstract_andrea_volkamer_cs3-2026.pdf)).

Walid presented a poster on his project, **“Computer-aided drug design of novel Elastase B inhibitors.”** 
([poster abstract](https://infochim.u-strasbg.fr/IMG/pdf/p44_mohammad_walid_shahrour_abstract_poster_cs3-2026.pdf)).

The summer school featured plenary lectures, oral presentations, poster sessions, and hands-on tutorials covering a broad range of topics in modern chemoinformatics. We especially enjoyed exchanging ideas with researchers from different backgrounds and discussing how computational approaches can contribute to the discovery of new therapeutic compounds.
